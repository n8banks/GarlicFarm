## Page 4: Astrum Empire

### Astrum Empire
> "By the Emperor's will, and the Stars Above!"

*(Margin note: "Empress'?")*

- A powerful faction with a diverse populace, the Astrum Empire values **merit** above all else, with a deep belief in the strength, morality, and righteousness of the Emperor.
- Highly organized and well supplied. Attacking directly will be inadvisable, as troops will synergize well and attack cooperatively.
- Valuing merit and morals, Astrum will be cautious with vampires, and very good reputation will be necessary for friendly relations.
- Are there dark secrets held within their dungeons? **TBD**
- No single organized religion, but most of the populace is devoted to the faction in addition to personal beliefs.

**This makes The Crown a Holy Relic.**

*[Sketches: a flag (a diamond containing a four-pointed star above a crown, with smaller stars on either side) and three alternative emblem designs: two round seals with stars, crown, and laurels, and a diamond badge.]*

## Page 5: Vampire mythos

### Vampire Mythos: Legends, Abilities, and Weaknesses

**Traits**
- Immortal
- Blood-feeder
- Nocturnal
- Undead
- Pale
- Cold
- No pulse
- Lean
- Fangs
- No reflection in mirrors
- No shadow
- Aversion to garlic
- Pretty/alluring
- Red eyes
- Ageless
- Ugly when hungry/angry
- Slight difference in smell
  - Not well liked by animals
- Pointy ears

**Abilities**
- Super strength
- Super durability
- Super speed
- Shapeshifting (mist, bats)
- Regeneration
- Super senses
- Normal disease immunity
- Cold resistance
- Night vision
- Mastery of darkness
- Hypnotism
- Invulnerability
- Magic affinity

**Weaknesses**
- Intense direct sunlight (weakness, burning, death)
- Stake to the heart (burning via hellfire and death)
- Fire
- Decapitation
- Blood deficiency
- Holy artifacts, blessed artifacts

**Quirks**
- Must be invited into private areas
- Weak during day
- Sleep in coffin
- Count grains of salt
- Can't cross running water
- Weak to religious symbolism/idolatry
- Silver
- Sleep on dirt from own grave

### Skill Tree
- Normal ability evolution requires experience and a surplus of blood
- A large excess of blood can trigger **Blood Lust**, which affects stats but will also allow access to the most powerful abilities

*[Sketch: three nodes in a chain with blood costs: 100 → 300 → 999. The 999 node is drawn with fangs.]*

*(Health does not increase by stats. This makes damage via weaknesses uniquely dangerous.)*
- Strength
- Resilience (damage resistance)
- Agility
- Regeneration
- Blood Satiety
